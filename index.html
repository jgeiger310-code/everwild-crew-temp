Our Story

Why Everwild matters to us

We’re Brittany, Sam, and Jake—three different people with our own personalities, talents, and ways of showing love, but one shared belief: leave people and places a little better than you found them. We’ve had the privilege of experiencing Everwild a few times now, and every trip has given us much more than a weekend of music. It has given us laughter, connection, creativity, and the kind of memories that stay with you long after the last set ends.

This time, we wanted to give a little of that energy back. We created this space to share photos, music, art, and the small moments that make Everwild feel so special. We appreciate the welcoming vibe of the festival and the people who arrive ready to dance, connect, support one another, and be fully themselves. This is our thank-you to the community that helped create so many great times—and our way of helping those moments and memories live on.

What this site is for

A home for the moments that made it special.

  • A polished place to share the vibe with other people
  • Music and community links in one spot
